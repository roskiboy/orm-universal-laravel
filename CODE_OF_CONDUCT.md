# Code of Conduct — orm-universal/laravel

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/es/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
summarized here. The full text prevails.

## Commitment

Ensure a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body diversity,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected

- Empathy and kindness.
- Respect for differing opinions and experiences.
- Constructive criticism and acceptance of it.
- Responsibility, apology, and learning from mistakes.
- Prioritizing the community's interest.

## Unacceptable

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Conduct reasonably inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and PRs that
violate these rules. Sanctions: warning, temporary or permanent expulsion, applied
consistently and in good faith.

## Reporting

In-Tech team private channel:

- https://github.com/roskiboy/orm-universal-laravel/security/advisories/new

All complaints are reviewed promptly and fairly, preserving the privacy and security of
the reporter.
